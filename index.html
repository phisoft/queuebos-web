How It Works

Simple for customers,
powerful for your business

1

Walk up to the kiosk

Customer walks up to the tablet, taps their desired service on the large touch-friendly screen — no staff needed.

2

Tap & get a ticket instantly

One tap — the built-in thermal printer issues a physical ticket with queue number, service name, and QR code for live tracking.

3

Staff serve & customers rate

Staff call the next number from their phone. TV display and voice announcements guide customers. After service, customers rate their experience.

Smart Features That

Self-Service Kiosk — All in One Device

One-tap service selection with instant ticket printing via built-in thermal printer. QR code on every ticket for live tracking. Company branding on screen and tickets. Touch-friendly large buttons for all ages.

Powerful Admin Dashboard & Analytics

Real-time statistics, bar charts by counter, doughnut charts by service. Service and counter management. Staff and user management with QR badge login. Full queue log history, audit trail, and data export/import — all from a web browser.

Learn More

Counter Controls on Any Phone

One-tap NEXT, HOLD & Recall, CALL functions. Works on any smartphone browser — Android or iPhone. No app install needed. Unlimited counters from the same queue pool.

Public TV Display & Voice TTS

Giant counter display for waiting areas — up to 4 counters with real-time updates. Audio chime on every call. Built-in Android TTS voice announcements. Works on any screen with a browser.

QR Badge Staff Login

Staff scan a printed QR badge to log in instantly — no typing. Desk sticker mode auto-assigns counters. Badges never expire. Printed on the same thermal printer the kiosk uses.
