How It Works

Simple for customers,
powerful for your business

1

Walk up to the kiosk

Customer walks up to the tablet, taps their desired service on the large touch-friendly screen — no staff needed.

2

Tap & get a ticket instantly

One tap — the built-in thermal printer issues a physical ticket with queue number, service name, and QR code for live tracking.

3

Staff serve & customers rate

Staff call the next number from their phone. TV display and voice announcements guide customers. After service, customers rate their experience.

Powerful Admin Dashboard

Counter Controls on Any Phone

Staff press Next, Hold, or Recall from their own smartphone — no special app needed. Works on any phone browser. Add as many counters as you need at no extra cost.

TV Display & Voice Announcements

Show live queue information on any TV screen in your waiting area. Up to 4 counters displayed at once. Built-in voice calls out the next number so customers always know when it's their turn.

QR Badge Staff Login

Print a QR badge for each staff member using the same printer. Staff scan their badge to log in instantly — no typing usernames or passwords. Badges never expire.
